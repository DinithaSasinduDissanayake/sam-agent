## Summary of Key Findings

### Retailer Coverage
- **SPAR2U** (online) has the widest selection — 62 frozen sausage products across all major brands. Best single source for comparison shopping.
- **Cargills / Cargills Online** — Primary seller of Goldi, Sam's, and Cargills Finest brands.
- **OnlineKade.lk** — Good selection of Keells Krest and Crescent products with competitive pricing.
- **Keells Meathouse** — Direct from Keells Food Products.
- **Kapruka** — Convenient for Goldi, Sam's, Bairaha products.
- **Janasiri.com** — Best source for Nelna products.
- **Maxies** — Direct sales from their own online shop.
- **Laugfs & Arpico** — No specific frozen sausage price data found online for these chains. They likely stock the same brands available at other retailers (Goldi, Sam's, Keells, etc.) but prices were not found.

### Quality / Availability Notes
1. **Pussalla** is noteworthy — their regular retail sausages won 15 awards (13 Gold, 1 Silver, 1 Bronze) at the DFV Global Meat Product Awards 2025 in Germany. They're a serious quality contender. [Source](https://pussalla.lk/blog/world-class-sausages-made-in-sri-lanka-pussalla-wins-global-recognition-at-iffa-2025)
2. **Your Butcher** products are almost entirely sold out on SPAR2U — may be discontinued or a limited run.
3. **Gills** has the widest variety (fish, chicken, bockwurst, kochchi, cheese & onion, garlic, pimentos) at competitive prices.
4. **Nelna** offers the lowest prices in the market, especially in 1kg catering/bulk packs.
5. **Maxies** has the lowest per-100g price for basic chicken sausages, especially when buying 1kg packs.
6. **Keells Krest** has SLS certification — the only sausage brand with this certification — and dominates the market with the widest product range. [Source](https://www.keellsfoods.lk/keells-krest/sausages.html)

### Nutritional Transparency
Very poor across the board. Only Keells Krest Chunky Chicken Sausages had any nutritional data publicly available (via a third-party calorie tracking site). Bairaha makes general health claims but no specific numbers. For pricing and nutrition info, physical packaging is the most reliable source.

### Leads for Further Investigation
1. **Beef sausages** — Only Elephant House, Crescent (Norfolk), and one brand on S&S Exports seem to offer beef sausages. Worth checking Cargills (Sara's brand) more closely.
2. **Cocktail sausages** — Only Your Butcher BBQ Cocktail (250g, sold out) and Robert Beef Cocktail (imported, at The Halal Food Shop) found. May be niche.
3. **Hot dogs** — S&S Exports has chicken and beef hot dogs, but these may be food-service only.
4. **Pork sausages** — Elephant House dominates here (pork sausages, pork lingus, pawkies).
5. **Import brands** — "Robert" sausages (beef cocktail with chicken, 425g) seen at The Halal Food Shop — likely imported, needs investigation.
6. **Cargills Finest** line appears to be a newer premium offering — prices and availability worth tracking.
