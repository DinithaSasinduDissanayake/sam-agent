## Notes & Observations

1. **Best value (per 100g):** Sara's 500g at Rs 133.50/100g (sale price on Glomark). Regular price Rs 178/100g. Keells 500g at Rs 800 (SPAR2U sale) = Rs 160/100g is also strong.

2. **Best documented quality:** Bairaha is the only brand with full nutrition data — no fillers, no binders, no breadcrumbs, no added hormones, antibiotics-free.

3. **Biggest brand:** Keells/Krest dominates with ~70% market share. Most size options and variants (plain, Kirata Mirisata, bulk).

4. **Same company, two brands:** Norfolk Foods produces both **Norfolk** (foodservice) and **Crescent** (retail). Similarly, Cargills produces both **Goldi** and **Sara's** (Goldi is the mainstream brand, Sara's is halal-certified).

5. **Beef option:** Only **Crescent** (by Norfolk) offers beef meatballs (200g, Rs 790). All others are chicken.

6. **Fish option:** **Lihini Dives** is the only fish meatball found (300g, Rs 500).

7. **Premium pricing:** Keells Kirata Mirisata (Rs 300/100g) and Pussalla Bite size (Rs 212/100g) are the priciest chicken options. Crescent beef at Rs 395/100g is most expensive overall.

8. **Missing data:** No retailer consistently lists ingredient lists or nutrition panels online. Would need in-store package photos for complete nutritional comparison.

9. **Websites to check for deeper info:** bairaha.com, keellsfoods.lk, cargillsceylon.com, pussalla.lk, norfolk.lk, prima.com.lk
